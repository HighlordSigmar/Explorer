  Instances.lua (dungeons, raids, world bosses, their levels, entrances and
  instance maps) is generated from InstanceJournal's database by

QUESTIE-OCTO contributors (MIT license)
  The zone level ranges in Zones.lua are the ones Questie-Octo audited

Diginfotek and Spartelfant - https://github.com/Spartelfant/LevelRange-Turtle

  its tables.

MODERNMAPMARKERS by tilr and Heallios - https://github.com/tilare/ModernMapMarkers
  The positions of the boats, zeppelins and the tram in Transports.lua were
